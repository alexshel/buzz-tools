File & Text Comparer

Paste two pieces of text into the panes and click Compare — line numbers and change highlights appear right in each pane, and the text stays editable for re-runs. The bar on the left maps every difference — click a marker to jump both panes to it — and you can drag the handle below the panes to resize them together. Panel names are editable too (handy for screenshots). Everything runs in your browser; nothing is ever uploaded.
